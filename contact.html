Before you reach out

Common first questions.

Is the first conversation really free?

Yes. No retainer, no fee for a conversation, no commitment that follows it. The first call or meeting exists so we can both decide whether the fit is right. If it isn't, I will often point you toward a colleague who is better suited.

I'm not sure I'm ready to move yet. Should I still call?

The earlier the conversation, the better the outcome. Several of my best client outcomes started eighteen to twenty-four months before the sale or purchase, with a market read, a pricing target, and a plan to prep the property properly. If you are a year out, that is the right time.

Do you work outside King City and York Region?

King City and York Region are the heart of the practice, and where the local fluency runs deepest. I also work into central Toronto for established clients with multi-property portfolios. If you are outside those areas, I am happy to refer you to a specialist I trust.

What's the best way to reach you?

Phone for time-sensitive questions, email for anything that benefits from a written record or attachments. I read every email and return every call personally. The office line at 905-731-2000 reaches the front desk first; mention you are trying to reach me directly and they will route you.

Can I just see what's on the MLS?

You can browse active listings any time at maryamhaghighi.royallepage.ca. The more interesting inventory in King City and the surrounding estate pockets often moves before it reaches the public board, so the listings you see online are usually only part of the picture worth discussing.
