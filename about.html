Expertise · Integrity · Smart Investments

Hi, I'm Maryam.

I'm a driven entrepreneur, real estate investor, and executive leader with over 20 years of experience across property investments, banking management, and tech startups. Now a King City specialist serving buyers, sellers, and investors across York Region and the Greater Toronto Area.

My background in engineering, business, and finance gives me an analytical edge in real estate. I bring that same rigour to every transaction, whether you're buying your first home in Aurora, selling a long-held estate in King City, or building an investment portfolio across Richmond Hill and Vaughan. I work hands-on and detail-oriented, supported by editorial photographers and marketing specialists, with targeted strategies that build demand on the right buyer profile, not just visibility.

My approach is deliberate. No countdown timers, no manufactured urgency. We start with a conversation about what you're trying to build, and work outward from there, with the numbers, the network, and the local fluency to back every recommendation.

Beyond real estate, I find fulfillment in travelling, exploring new cuisines through cooking, and spending quality time with my family and friends. I'm proud to support Toronto SickKids Hospital as part of my ongoing commitment to community philanthropy.
