# Journal voice guide

The Journal is editorial, not promotional. Each post should sound
like Maryam thinking out loud about her own market for the kind of
reader who's making a real decision about a real home.

## Positioning

- **Maryam is a King City resident and York Region specialist** with
  20+ years across business, real estate, banking, and investment.
  This isn't a generic GTA blog. Lead with King City whenever the
  topic allows; if it doesn't fit King City directly, lead with York
  Region. Toronto/GTA is the broader frame.
- **The reader is buying, selling, or investing in this corridor**.
  Assume some financial literacy. Don't condescend, but don't assume
  insider language either — explain TRREB, CMHC, NRST, stress test,
  cap rate, etc. on first use.

## Tone

- **Deliberate**, **expert**, **locally rooted**. Confident without
  being performative. The voice of a senior practitioner, not a
  marketer.
- **No hype**. No "amazing", "incredible", "stunning", "skyrocket",
  "explode", "shocking", "must-read", "you won't believe", "game-changer".
- **No false urgency**. No countdown framing, no "act now before it's
  too late", no manufactured FOMO.
- **No "quiet" as a brand word**. Use *deliberate*, *considered*,
  *composed*, *measured*, *unhurried* if the meaning needs to land.
- **Numbers behind claims**. If you say the market shifted, cite the
  shift (price band, volume change, days-on-market, rate move). If a
  fact is fuzzy, say so explicitly ("anecdotally", "in my own deals
  this quarter").
- **Second person is fine.** "You" addressing the reader is welcome
  in advisory passages. Avoid royal "we" unless you're literally
  describing the firm's process.

## Language

- **Canadian English** spelling: neighbourhood, behaviour, realise,
  modelling, organisation, centre, cheque, kilometre. (Note: most
  existing posts mix US and CA spelling. New posts should standardise
  on CA.)
- **Active voice.** "The Bank of Canada raised rates," not "Rates
  were raised by the Bank of Canada."
- **No em-dashes**. Use commas, periods, colons, semicolons, or
  parentheses instead. This is a site-wide rule, not a stylistic
  preference.
- **Single quotes for nested quotation, double for outer**: she said
  the buyer "had a 'wait and see' attitude" about the offer.
- **Numerals for 10+, words for 0–9** (standard journalism style).
  Always numerals for money, percentages, and dates.

## Structure

A standard post is **800–1,100 words**. Use this skeleton:

1. **Lede** (2–3 sentences). State the question or shift the post is
   about. Anchor it in a specific local observation if possible
   ("Three King City listings sold above asking last week. Here's
   what that signals.").
2. **Three to five subheads** (`## H2`, occasionally `### H3`).
   Each subhead opens a distinct angle. Don't structure with "Pros /
   Cons" lists unless the topic genuinely is a comparison.
3. **A short closer** (2–3 sentences). Land on what the reader
   should do or watch for. Avoid a hard CTA. The site itself has
   contact CTAs; the Journal exists to build credibility, not to
   convert.

## What to avoid

- Listicles ("7 reasons to ...") — Maryam's Journal isn't BuzzFeed.
- "In conclusion" / "in summary" headers — the closer doesn't need a
  label.
- Generic stock phrases ("real estate is a journey", "your dream
  home", "now is a great time to buy").
- Reproducing more than ~15 words from any external source.
- Specific competitor names (other brokerages, individual agents).
- Tax or legal advice. Refer the reader to their accountant or
  lawyer if a topic crosses that line.
- Lifestyle filler ("imagine yourself sipping coffee on the patio").

## Local references worth weaving in

Use these where they fit naturally — never as keyword stuffing.

- **King City**: Kingscross, Kingsview, Hills of St. Andrew, the
  village core along King Road, the estate concessions north of the
  15th Sideroad, Eaton Hall, the King Township municipal services
  (water, septic considerations on larger lots), Seneca King Campus,
  proximity to the Oak Ridges Moraine.
- **Aurora**: Bayview Northeast, Aurora Estates, the Yonge Street
  heritage strip, the GO station commute.
- **Richmond Hill**: Bayview Hill, Mill Pond, Oak Ridges, Jefferson.
- **Vaughan**: Kleinburg, Maple, VMC and the subway extension.
- **Newmarket**: Stonehaven, Summerhill Estates, Main Street historic.
- **Markham**: Unionville, Cathedraltown, Cornell, Berczy.
