Buyer journey

Buying with me.

Four steps, no surprises. The whole point is for you to feel like the decision was always yours.

01

A conversation, not a pitch

We start over coffee. You tell me about the life you're building. I listen and ask better questions than the budget one.

02

A short list, curated

I'll send you a small handful of homes that fit — including pocket listings the public board doesn't see.

03

Tour with intent

We visit together. I point out what to love, what to negotiate down, and what should make you walk.

04

Offer and close

I write the offer, I negotiate it, I handle the paperwork. You sign at the end and pick up keys.
